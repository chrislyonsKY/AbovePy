# Attribution

## KyFromAbove Program

The data accessed through abovepy is collected and published by the
**KyFromAbove program**, a statewide initiative to acquire high-resolution
aerial imagery and LiDAR elevation data for the Commonwealth of Kentucky.

- Website: [https://kyfromabove.ky.gov](https://kyfromabove.ky.gov)
- Program authority: Kentucky Division of Geographic Information (DGI)
- Parent agency: Commonwealth Office of Technology, Commonwealth of Kentucky

## Data Providers

KyFromAbove data is produced through partnerships between state, federal, and
local agencies. Key contributors include:

- **Kentucky Division of Geographic Information** -- program management and data
  distribution
- **USGS / 3DEP** -- federal partnership for LiDAR acquisition
- **FEMA** -- floodplain mapping and elevation data
- **Kentucky Transportation Cabinet** -- infrastructure and corridor data
- **Local governments** -- cost-share partners for aerial acquisition

## Data Licensing

KyFromAbove data is **public government data** made available for unrestricted
use. As products of Kentucky state government, these datasets are in the public
domain and carry no copyright restrictions.

## Suggested Citation

When publishing work that uses KyFromAbove data accessed through abovepy:
